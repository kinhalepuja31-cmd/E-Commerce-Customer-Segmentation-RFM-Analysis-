# E-Commerce-Customer-Segmentation-RFM-Analysis-



#Project Overview

Not all customers shop the same way. If an online store sends identical marketing emails to a loyal VIP buyer, a brand-new shopper, and someone who hasn't bought anything in a year, it wastes advertising money.
This project solves that problem using a powerful hybrid approach:

1. Python (Google Colab): Used for advanced data cleaning, scaling, and using unsupervised machine learning (K-Means Clustering) to find hidden customer groups.

2. Power BI Desktop: Used to build a modern, interactive dashboard to present those machine learning groups to business managers.


# What is RFM Analysis?

To group our customers, we tracked three core buying behaviors:

1. Recency (R): How many days ago did the customer buy something?

2. Frequency (F): How many separate orders did they place?

3. Monetary Value (M): How much total money did they spend?


# Key Business Insights Discovered

Our machine learning model automatically split the customer database into 4 clear business groups: 

• 🥇 Champions / VIPs (56.5% of total revenue): A small group of highly loyal shoppers who buy frequently and spend heavily. They bring in more than half of the store's money ($5.07M)!

• 🌟 New Shoppers: Fresh customers who placed an order within the last 15 days. They need a welcome coupon to turn them into regular buyers.

• ⚠️ At-Risk Big Spenders: Customers who spent thousands of dollars in the past but haven't bought anything in months. They need immediate "We Miss You" discounts before they leave permanently.

• 📉 Inactive / Lost Customers: Casual shoppers who bought one cheap item a long time ago and disappeared.
